Taught by someone who's sat exactly where you're sitting.

House of French - Maison Française - is a private coaching practice founded and run by Prabhleen.

Prabhleen built this practice out of her own experience immigrating to Canada and preparing for her own TEF exam - learning a new language while everything else in her life was new too. That's the spirit House of French is taught in: meeting students where they are, and making French feel approachable rather than intimidating.

It's her practice, and she teaches every student personally.

Choose your starting point.

Wherever you're starting from - brand new to French, or brushing up after years away - there's a track built around it.

  • Beginner

    For complete beginners starting from zero - the foundations of French pronunciation, everyday vocabulary, and simple conversation.

  • A1

    The starting point of the CEFR scale: understanding and using familiar everyday expressions, and introducing yourself with simple questions and answers.

  • A2

    Describing your surroundings, talking about past and future events, and handling routine everyday situations with more ease.

  • B1

    Discussing familiar topics, sharing your opinion, and understanding the main points of everyday conversations and texts.

  • B2

    Following detailed discussions, understanding nuance, and communicating clearly and confidently in both work and everyday contexts.

  • C1

    Understanding long, complex material and expressing yourself fluently and naturally, without searching for words.

  • Fast-Track Course

    An intensive version of private coaching for students working against a closer deadline - the same 1:1 attention, on a condensed timeline.

  • TEF/TCF Prep

    Focused, exam-specific practice - getting familiar with the TEF Canada and TCF Canada format, timing, and question types.

  • Tailored

    Built around your specific goal or gap - whichever combination of listening, speaking, reading, or writing needs the most attention.
